Ground and surfaces

  • marble The ground behind each panel, with tile joints.
  • cloth Panels, quiet buttons and answer options.
  • sunken Notes and notices.
  • edge The outline and the lip of each cloth control. It reaches 3:1 on marble and cloth.

Text

  • ink Text on marble, cloth and sunken.
  • ink-muted Captions and labels.

Kiswah and gold

  • kiswah The panel header, the recitation block, a chosen answer. Nothing else is dark.
  • on-kiswah Text on the kiswah. on-kiswah-muted is for the meaning line.
  • gold Act here: the main button, the current bead, the switch that is on. One flat colour, never a gradient.
  • gold-edge The lip under a gold button. gold-line is the outline of a gold control and of a right answer.
  • on-gold Text on gold.

Mistake

  • mistake A wrong answer: its outline, its cross and its words. Always with an icon and plain words.
  • mistake-tint The fill of a wrong answer.
