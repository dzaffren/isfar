# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format is based
on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres
to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- See the Kaabah and the mataf in 3D, with the first step in Malay or English.
- Switch between BM and EN, and the site keeps the choice on the device.
- Read the step when the device cannot show 3D, or try again when the 3D download fails.
- Walk through tawaf in 15 steps in a 3D mataf, with Next and Back, the arrow keys, a link to each step, and a top-view map when the device cannot show 3D.
- Check what you learned after the walk: seven questions on where each step happens and what to do there, with right or wrong after each answer and the steps to go over at the end.
- Walk through sa'i in 13 steps in a 3D mas'a, from Safa to the end of walk 7 at Marwah. A switch in the top bar moves between tawaf and sa'i.
- Check what you learned after the sa'i walk: seven questions on where each step of sa'i happens and what to do there, from step 13 or the link `#semak-sai`.
- Walk through ihram in 8 steps in a 3D Bir Ali, from the bath and the clothes to the talbiyah on the bus, with the niat and the talbiyah in Arabic. The switch in the top bar now holds Ihram, Tawaf and Sa'i, after BM and EN, and takes its own row on a phone.
- Check what you learned after the ihram walk: seven questions on what to do at each place of Bir Ali, from step 8 or the link `#semak-ihram`. On step 8, "Teruskan ke tawaf" now sits in the step panel.

### Fixed

- The mas'a now shows two one-way lanes with a low barrier between them, and each sa'i walk keeps to its own lane, in 3D and on the map.
